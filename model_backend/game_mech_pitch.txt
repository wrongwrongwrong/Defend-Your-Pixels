Yuxuan's Game Mech Pitch
Keywords: turn-based, grid map, strategy game
1. The Map & Terrain
The battlefield is a 12X12 grid designed to facilitate a three-stage game flow: Expansion, Contention, and Decisive Battle.
	•	Deployment Zones: Each player starts at opposite ends with 2–3 "Safe Resource Nodes" nearby for a steady early-game income.
	•	Command Tower: Has an HP of 20. Can regenerate HP by spending Ethers.
	•	The Mid-Field (The Conflict Zone): Five high-yield Ether Drills (Resource Points) are arranged in a 2-1-2 formation. These are the primary objectives.
	•	Terrain Types:
	•	Highways: Strategic paths connecting bases to the mid-field. Movement cost is halved (0.5 per grid), but units are in "transportation"(cannot use "attack/defend" ability).
	•	Fortresses/Mountains: High-ground areas that cost 2 movement points to enter but provide +1 Range for Attacker or reduced Shield energy consumption for Defender.
	•	Obstacles: Terrain pre-exist on Ether Drills or generated by Defenders. Cannot be passed through. Can be destroyed with 2/1 shoot.
	•	Movement Rule: Orthogonal only (Up, Down, Left, Right). No diagonal movement is permitted in the current prototype to ensure clear tactical positioning.
2. Units: The "Attacker" & The "Defender"
Units are physical markers. Their orientation (rotation) and physical state dictate their digital actions.
Universal Rules:
Each units have 1 action point. The unit can choose to spend the AP on the following actions:
	•	Movement: An unit gains 2 movement point with no upgrade. Cost: no Ether *.
	•	Use Ability: See Attack and Defender. Cost: 1 Ether*.
	•	Venting: Players can skip a turn to reduce Heat by 100%. Cost: no Ether.
	•	Capture: Only available when above a Ether Drill. Turn the drill into player's resource node. Cost: 1 Ether.
	•	Push: Push an enemy unit next to the unit 1 tile back if the space behind it is empty. Cost: 1 Ether*.
*may cost more or less ether with upgrade. That can be explored later.
A. The Attacker
	•	Keywords: Offensive ability, objective destruction.
	•	Basic Ability:The attacker can fire bullet with line of fire( which can be blocked by shield/obstacle) or projectile(the can bypass shield/obstacle).
	•	Evolution Path: Attackers can upgrade through three tiers (2/2/3 destroyed/captured objective) with a randomized traits as following:
	•	Mortar: Arcing shots that bypass obstacles or Defenders.
	•	Flamethrower: Piercing damage that hits 3–4 consecutive grids.
	•	Double-Tap: The ability to fire twice per turn at the cost of movement.
	•	Hit and run: The ability to move and fire.
	•	Attacker's Heat System: Attack generate Heat. For every bullet the attacker fired, Heat guage of the unit increase by 33%.
	•	Overload: If Heat reaches 100%, players can continue shooting. This requires a Dice Roll: A fail (1-2) results in a 2-turn paralysis; a success (3-6) means the attempt is successful but if the player want to go overload consecutively, they will suffer from a -1 penalty(fail will become (1-3) for 2nd attempt, (1-4) for 3rd, etc).
B. The Defender (The Shield)
	•	Role: Area denial and tactical deception.
	•	Base Stats: Can generate shield/obstacle in a range. Shield will be temporal and obstacle will be permenant.
	•	Defender's Heat System: Creating the obstacle generates Heat. For every obstacle the defender created, Heat guage of the unit increase by 33%.
	•	Overload: If Heat reaches 100%, players can force the shield to stay up. This requires a Dice Roll: A fail (1-2) results in a 2-turn paralysis; a success (3-6) means the generation of obstacle is success but if the player want to go overload consecutively, they will suffer from a -1 penalty(fail will become (1-3) for 2nd attempt, (1-4) for 3rd, etc).
Note:The heat& vent system is designed so players must pace their dilivery of firepower and prevent "snowballing". Even if one player captures a lot of Ether Drills(see Part3), a unit with 200% guage still suffer from overload even with 1 rest, encouraging heat management and slow down the wave of offensive.
3. Macro Economy & Victory Conditions
We move away from traditional "Action Points", which is free, turn-based resource, to an Ether-based Economy to ensure a powerful "Late-Game" feel.
	•	Ether (Energy): Movement is free, but Combat Actions (Shooting, Shielding, Ultimates) cost Ether.
	•	The Resource Flow: Capture Ether Drills to increase your per-turn energy income. Losing drills doesn't limit your ability to move, but it limits your "Firepower," preventing a total "snowball" effect while still rewarding map control.
	•	Victory Conditions:
	•	Elimination: Destroy the opponent's Command Tower (Core Objective).
	•	Dominance: Be the first to reach 100 Ethers through sustained control of Ether Drills.
