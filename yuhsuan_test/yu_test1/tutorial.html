⭐   HOW TO PLAY   ⭐

Old Mick Against the Mob — 1932 Great Emu War

🎯   Your Mission

You are Old Mick, an Australian farmer defending your homestead against a mob of rampaging emus.

Win the war by either of the following:

💥 Destroy the enemy HQ — hidden inside one of their target cells. Instant win.
🔥 Raze 30 enemy target cells via attrition.

Each side has 20 attackable target cells (highlighted orange / green). Cells outside these zones are safe — rays pass straight through them.

🔄   How a Turn Works

  1. Place your markers in your territory. 2× ATK (point in firing direction, 8 compass dirs)  ·  1× DEF (creates a 3×3 protected zone, cells inside need 2 hits)
  2. Flip the TURN marker to your opponent's side. This signals "I'm done — resolve my turn."
  3. Watch the resolution. Your two attacks fire automatically. Damage and destruction are shown on screen.
  4. Opponent does the same. Repeat until someone wins.

🎨   What You See on the Board

Dark orange — your target cells
Dark green — enemy target cells
Pale yellow — your safe zone
Pale green — enemy safe zone
🪵
HARD terrain (blocks rays)
SOFT terrain (2 hits to clear)
⚒
Damaged cell (still alive)
✕
Destroyed (scorched earth)
★
HQ revealed — you've found them!

💡   Strategy Tips

  • Place DEF wisely — cells in its 3×3 zone need 2 hits to destroy. Cover where you suspect the enemy will aim.
  • Don't react to attacks near your HQ — your opponent can read your face. Stay calm.
  • Track the enemy's ATK direction — their pointing tells you which of your cells they think are valuable.
  • The last unscarred cell on the enemy side is probably their HQ. Save your firepower for the kill shot.
  • Tier up when you can — Tier 2 enlarges your DEF zone to 5×5.
